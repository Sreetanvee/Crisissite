Error Analysis

Most misclassifications occur in low-evidence-completeness cases, particularly where only citizen reports are available without corroborating sensor or satellite data. Reviewers are advised to weight single-source assessments cautiously.

Known Failure Modes

  • Reduced satellite reliability under heavy cloud cover
  • Sensor dropout during power outages
  • Citizen reports may duplicate or conflict without context
  • Model has not been evaluated on non-flood disaster types

Dataset & Limitations SIMULATED

All data in this prototype is simulated.

Dataset Overview

This prototype uses a synthetic dataset built to demonstrate the system's decision-support architecture and human-review workflow for a simulated flood scenario. No real incident, sensor, or citizen data is used.

Data Sources

  • Simulated satellite imagery tiles
  • Simulated water-level and infrastructure sensors
  • Simulated citizen incident reports
  • Simulated regional weather signals

Evidence Modalities

Four modalities are fused per incident: imagery, sensor telemetry, citizen reports, and weather context, each carrying an independent confidence weight before fusion.

Data Processing

Simulated evidence streams are combined through a mock multimodal fusion step that produces a confidence and uncertainty estimate, along with a priority level surfaced to the human review queue.

Limitations

  • Metrics and confidence scores are illustrative, not derived from a validated model
  • The map is a stylized layout, not tied to real geography
  • No integration with live emergency-service systems

Known Failure Modes

See Model Evaluation for evidence-type-specific failure modes, including satellite cloud interference and sensor dropout.

Ethical / Safety Considerations

This platform is designed to keep a human reviewer in the decision loop at all times. It surfaces evidence, confidence, and uncertainty to support — never replace — operational judgment. No feature in this prototype authorizes automated action.
